How this works

Paste two PMS exports below. The tool figures out who's eligible for breakfast on the breakfast date you select — the morning after check-in through the morning of check-out, plus any day-use bookings.

📌 Important: The "Breakfast Date" is the morning the breakfast is served, not the date guests checked in. Tonight's in-house guests eat breakfast tomorrow morning — so to prepare for the next service, choose tomorrow's date.

  1. Source A — Check-in/out Report: start date must be earlier than your earliest in-house guest's check-in. For long-stay guests, push back several months.
  2. Source B — Reservation Report: cover roughly 14 days before to 14 days after today.
  3. Pick the breakfast date, click ↻ Recalculate, review the list, type any per-room remarks, then print or export.
⚠ Accuracy: Numbers come straight from the PMS. Front office must enter the actual adult/child count for every booking. If a count is wrong, fix it in the PMS — not on the printout. Accurate occupancy data feeds room revenue, housekeeping, statistics, and audit. The breakfast list is the symptom; PMS hygiene is the cure.
🌙 Watch out: post-night-audit reservations. This report is only as current as the PMS exports used to generate it. If a reservation is added or modified after night audit — and the report has already been printed — that change will not appear on the printed list.

Daily control point for the front desk: before handing the list to the kitchen, check the PMS for any reservations or modifications made after night audit. If any exist, either:
  • Re-export the data, click ↻ Recalculate, and reprint, or
  • Manually write the additions/changes on the printout and communicate them to the kitchen.

This usually doesn't happen often, but the 30-second check protects the kitchen from surprises.
✓ No archiving needed: Generate, use, discard. The PMS is the single source of truth — as long as you keep up with daily PMS backups, any past day's list can be regenerated on demand. Don't save printouts or PDFs. Pull live, use live, throw away.
